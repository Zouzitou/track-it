# Design critique

The main risk was a generic black-and-neon AI dashboard. Track it excludes acid green,
ornamental grids, gradient headlines, glass cards, fake HUD language, pills everywhere,
and repeated floating surfaces. Cobalt is reserved for selection and trace evidence.

The edge echo earns its visual strength by explaining temporal change. Coral and teal are
therefore excluded from navigation and panel decoration. The surrounding interface remains
quiet, rectilinear, dense, and instrument-like.

Host Grotesk replaces the initial Archivo/Inter pairing at the user's direction. Its uniwidth
metrics suit changing editor labels and numeric-adjacent controls without creating a generic
system-font appearance; JetBrains Mono stays limited to actual data.

The supplied raw guidance includes web/mobile conventions that do not map literally to a Qt
desktop editor. Applicable principles—semantic tokens, clear focus, honest progress, SVG
icons, reduced motion, stable interaction states—remain. Desktop keyboard workflows,
resizable panels, and a custom virtualized timeline take precedence.

Generic defaults deliberately avoided: cream/serif editorial styling, newspaper layouts,
gratuitous glass, giant marketing type, decorative numbering, emoji icons, and copied assets
from commercial editors.
